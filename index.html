HOW TO INSTALL ANDROID APK

4 easy steps to get the App, Widgets & Wallpaper

1
Download Android APK (.apk) Tap the green button below to directly download lilos-garden.apk from our official GitHub release (no GitHub account or login required).
2
Open Downloaded APK Once downloaded, tap the download notification or open your phone's Downloads folder and tap lilos-garden.apk (no unzipping needed).
3
Allow Android Installation If your phone shows a standard Play Protect / Unknown Apps prompt, tap "Allow from this source" or "Install anyway".
4
Add Home Screen Widgets & Wallpaper! Open the app, then long-press an empty space on your Android Home Screen → tap Widgets → choose Lilo's Garden!

SANCTUARY STORY & CREDITS

The heart behind Lilo's Garden

Cozy Companion & Utility App

Lilo's Garden was born out of deep love and remembrance. Lilo (the pure white cat with bright blue eyes and collar) and Cheeto (the warm white cat with captivating amber and blue heterochromia) were more than just cats—they were inseparable brothers whose playful spirits and comforting purrs brought warmth to every day.

In this eternal pixel sanctuary, winter never chills their paws, rain is gentle and refreshing, and bowls of warm soup and fish are forever replenished. Here, they wander freely beneath open skies, waiting to greet you whenever you need a peaceful breath.
